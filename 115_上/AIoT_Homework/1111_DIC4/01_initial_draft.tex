\title{Context-Aware Restaurant Recommendation with Taichung Bus Accessibility: An AI Agent Proposal}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Jin-De Lin, Yan-Zhe Gu, Yu-Jie Wei, and Chun-Yu Yeh}\IEEEauthorblockA{National Chung Hsing University, Taichung, Taiwan}}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Restaurant recommendation must satisfy dining preferences and practical transportation restrictions. This first IEEE conference draft proposes context-first restaurant retrieval, TDX bus analysis, and evidence-based three-valued constraints. It is a proposal; experiments have not yet been conducted.
\end{abstract}
\begin{IEEEkeywords}AI agent, restaurant recommendation, public transportation\end{IEEEkeywords}
\section{Introduction}
A restaurant may be semantically appropriate but inaccessible by bus in time for a planned meal. We propose verifying transportation feasibility before the final restaurant ranking.
\section{Related Work}
Language-agent planning, semantic retrieval, subjective preferences, and public transit routing motivate the integrated research problem. The comprehensive literature table is included in the revised draft.
\section{Design}
Nine modules cover offline source preparation, entity resolution and storage, then online requirement parsing, semantic retrieval, evidence filtering, bus analysis, three-valued checking, and explained recommendation.
\section{Evaluation Plan}
Future comparisons will examine BM25, vector, and hybrid retrieval and ranking with or without transit constraints.
\section{Conclusion}
This initial draft identifies the problem and system scope without claiming empirical results.
